# Statistical appendix

Analysis date: 2026-10-19 (Asia/Shanghai). Fixed development selection: 128 training images from 6471, selection seed 20261014. Two distinct frozen training strategies, one epoch100 seed0 checkpoint each. Six views are repeated measurements, not six independent samples. No independent training replicates, test-set AP, optimizer updates, or training-speed observations are available in this campaign.

Native loss: actual E2EDetectLoss return value summed, own assignments for every arm. Conditional query sampling is analytically enumerated at p=.25. No clipping or optimizer transformation. Conditional expectation equality does not establish unbiased Adam updates or convergence. This is batch1 eval mode, not actual minibatch train mode. R compares query budgets, not equal wall-clock cost.

Primary decision: original locked gate, R<1 for all four head/scale cells of a deployable control. All12 deployment cells fail. Oracle arms never participate in candidate eligibility. Ratios use sums of paired energies; zero denominators are not replaced with arbitrary constants. All total-vector energies independently match the sum of seven parameter-block energies. Selected-outcome norm is reconstructed from ||4ge-3ga||² as an independent arithmetic check.

Spatial statistics: exact hard matching/common denominator; cls and box outputs are separate from true parameter derivatives. P3 localization includes IoU and the reg_max=1 auxiliary normalized ltrb L1. Each image averages its two flips for each scale/head/arm. Cosine, paired mean difference, SD, median, quartiles and direction counts are reported. Native gradient metrics and hard-assignment metrics are not the same objective.

Uncertainty: 46 filename-prefix groups;2000 resamples, seed20261019. Groups are resampled jointly for numerator and denominator (or paired differences). Means retain image weights by dividing sums by resampled image counts. Reported2.5/97.5 percentiles are descriptive, pointwise sensitivity intervals conditional on this sample and these heads. Filename prefixes are not verified independent videos. We do not claim population95% coverage. No training-seed p-values, omnibus ranking, equivalence, or multiplicity-adjusted significance claims are valid; no inferential test is performed. The many pointwise intervals are not simultaneous confidence bands. Heavy tails are retained and reported with median/quartiles, max-share and leave-top-k sensitivity; no trimming enters the locked decision.

Post-hoc scalar oracle: one constant per head/scale fitted on these same128 images using pooled inner products. It minimizes conditional variance within that scalar class only. Its apparent gain is optimistic and not a held-out prediction, cost result, or revised pass decision. Applying a fitted alpha to the same query outcome during real training could violate the independence needed for the estimator; an actual implementation would require independent/past calibration and additional validation.

Raw input/output hashes and old-arm replication are in verification.json. There were18432 shared output rows with exact zero change in prior classification cosine, localization cosine and native loss. Full CSV tables accompany the report; figures were inspected as PNG after generating PDF/SVG/PNG from the same tables.
